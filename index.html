Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

禁果 Ginkgo — Short Drama App Lock

Last updated / 最後更新：October 22, 2026 (v2)

本政策說明 禁果 Ginkgo（以下簡稱「本 App」）如何處理您的資訊。我們承諾保護您的隱私。

This Privacy Policy describes how 禁果 Ginkgo ("the App", "we", "us") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Data We Collect / 我們收集的資料

本 App 不建立帳號、不要求登入，也不收集任何個人資料。我們沒有伺服器，您在 App 中的所有資料都只存在您的裝置上，或您自己的 iCloud 帳號中。

The App does not create accounts, does not require login, and does not collect any personal data. We have no servers. All of your data stays on your device, or in your own iCloud account.

2. Screen Time / 螢幕使用時間

本 App 使用 Apple 的 Screen Time 框架（Family Controls、Managed Settings、Device Activity）鎖住您選擇的 App 與網站：

The App uses Apple's Screen Time frameworks (Family Controls, Managed Settings, Device Activity) to block the apps and websites you choose:

  • 授權：首次使用時，系統會請您允許「螢幕使用時間」權限，本 App 只用來管理您自己的裝置
    Authorization: on first use, the system asks you to allow Screen Time access. The App only uses it to manage your own device
  • 選擇的 App：您選擇的 App 由系統以加密的代號保存在裝置上，我們看不到是哪些 App
    Selected apps: the apps you pick are stored on-device as encrypted tokens by the system. We cannot see which apps they are
  • 使用時間：每日上限由系統在裝置上計算，本 App 只會收到「已達上限」的通知，無法得知您實際的使用時間或使用了哪些 App
    Usage time: daily limits are measured by the system on-device. The App is only told when a limit is reached and cannot see your actual usage or which apps you used

3. Data Storage & Retention / 資料儲存與保留

  • 您設定的規則（每日上限、冷靜期、嚴格模式等）僅儲存於您的裝置本機
    Your rules (daily limits, cooldown, strict mode, etc.) are stored only on your device
  • 每次解鎖的時間與打開被鎖 App 的次數僅存於本機，用來計算當天的額度與果實，保留 30 天後自動刪除
    Unlock times and how often you opened a blocked app are stored only on-device to calculate the day's limits and fruit, and are automatically deleted after 30 days
  • 禁果園中每天的結算結果（金果、果實、落葉）儲存於本機
    Daily results in the garden (golden fruit, fruit, withered leaf) are stored on-device
  • 未開啟 iCloud 同步時，刪除 App 即永久刪除所有本機資料
    Without iCloud sync, deleting the App permanently removes all local data
  • 我們沒有伺服器，無法存取您的任何資料
    We have no servers and cannot access any of your data

4. iCloud Sync / iCloud 同步

  • 開啟 iCloud 同步時，禁果園的結算結果與您的規則會存在您自己的 iCloud 帳號中，由 Apple 依其隱私權政策保管
    With iCloud sync on, your garden results and rules are stored in your own iCloud account, kept by Apple under its privacy policy
  • 您選擇的 App 清單與每次解鎖的紀錄不會同步
    Your selected apps and individual unlock records are not synced
  • 我們無法存取您 iCloud 中的資料。您可以隨時在 App 的設定中關閉同步
    We cannot access the data in your iCloud. You can turn sync off at any time in the App's settings

5. Notifications / 通知

本 App 只在您於被鎖的 App 上按下「我真的要看…」或「付費解鎖…」時發送本機通知，帶您回到本 App 解鎖。我們不會發送行銷或廣告通知。

The App only sends a local notification when you tap "I really want to watch…" or "Paid unlock…" on a blocked app, to bring you back to the App to unlock. We never send marketing or advertising notifications.

6. Camera / 相機

只有在您把「比 YA」設為解鎖關卡時，本 App 才會在解鎖畫面請求使用前鏡頭，用來確認您在眼睛旁比出 YA 手勢。畫面只在您的裝置上即時分析，不會拍照、錄影、儲存或上傳。沒有選這個關卡時，本 App 不會使用相機。

Only if you add the "Peace sign" step to your unlock steps does the App ask to use the front camera on the unlock screen, to check that you are making a peace sign next to your eye. The camera feed is analyzed in real time on your device only; it is never photographed, recorded, stored or uploaded. If you do not choose this step, the App never uses the camera.

7. Game Center Leaderboard / Game Center 排行榜

排行榜為選用功能，預設關閉。您在設定中加入後，本 App 會透過 Apple 的 Game Center 回報您累積的果實數，排名與您的 Game Center 暱稱由 Apple 依其隱私權政策處理。您隨時可以在設定中離開排行榜。

The leaderboard is optional and off by default. If you join it in Settings, the App reports your total fruit count through Apple's Game Center; rankings and your Game Center nickname are handled by Apple under its privacy policy. You can leave the leaderboard at any time in Settings.

8. In-App Purchase / App 內購買

「偷嚐禁果」付費解鎖為選用功能，預設關閉。付款完全由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得您的付款資訊，也不會將購買紀錄與您的身分連結。

The "Sneak a Bite" paid unlock is optional and off by default. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment information and do not link purchases to your identity.

9. What We Do Not Do / 我們不做的事

  • 不建立帳號或收集識別資訊 / We do not create accounts or collect identifying information
  • 不上傳任何資料至我們的伺服器 / We do not upload any data to our servers
  • 不查看您選擇了哪些 App 或您的使用時間 / We do not see which apps you select or your usage time
  • 不追蹤您的位置 / We do not track your location
  • 不嵌入任何廣告或分析 SDK / We do not embed any advertising or analytics SDKs
  • 不與任何第三方共享資料 / We do not share data with any third parties

10. Children / 兒童

本 App 不向任何人收集個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

The App does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

若我們對隱私權政策進行重大變更，將更新本頁面頂端的「最後更新」日期。繼續使用本 App 即表示接受更新後的政策。

If we make material changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
